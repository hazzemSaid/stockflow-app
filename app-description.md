# MakhzanFlow (ستوك فلو)

**Short description:** MakhzanFlow is an Arabic-first, multi-tenant warehouse and supermarket distribution management system that enables small and medium businesses in the MENA region to manage inventory, invoicing, customers, payments, and team permissions from a single mobile app.

## Full Description

### What problem the app solves
Small and medium businesses in the Arab world typically rely on paper records, disconnected tools, or English-first software to manage their operations. This leads to fragmented inventory tracking, untracked customer debts, no expiration-date monitoring, uncontrolled employee access, and no unified view across multiple branches or warehouses. MakhzanFlow replaces all of these disconnected workflows with a single, Arabic-first mobile platform.

### Main features
- **Multi-company (multi-tenant) management** — Create and switch between multiple companies from one account, with an invite-code system for joining existing businesses.
- **Inventory & product management** — Full CRUD with images, expiration dates, quantity adjustments, movement history, and low-stock alerts.
- **Customer management** — Track customers with store details, debt balances, payment history, transaction logs, and filtering by payment status (paid, partial, deferred).
- **Invoicing & sales** — Create invoices with a barcode-supported product picker, support for cash/partial/credit payments, fixed or percentage discounts, payment tracking per invoice, and print/share capabilities.
- **Payments & receivables** — Record and track payments against invoices with support for cash, bank transfer, and Meeza payment methods.
- **Role-based permission system** — Granular, owner-controlled permissions across 6 sections (dashboard, products, customers, invoices, payments, reports) with a `PermissionGate` widget that conditionally shows/hides UI elements per employee.
- **Team management** — Owner can invite members, manage join requests, deactivate/reactivate/remove members, and promote/demote owners.
- **Dashboard & reports** — KPI overview (products, customers, debt, sales), weekly sales charts, recent activity feed, quick action buttons, and Excel export.

### Who should use it
- Warehouse and distribution company owners in the Arab world
- Supermarket and wholesale store managers
- Pharmacies, restaurants, and retail shops needing inventory tracking
- Multi-branch businesses that need unified management
- Teams requiring granular employee permission controls
- Small to medium enterprises (SMEs) in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Jordan, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria, Yemen, Libya, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco, and Sudan

### Why it is useful
MakhzanFlow is purpose-built for Arabic-speaking business owners who need a simple, all-in-one mobile solution to run their operations. It eliminates the overhead of juggling multiple tools by combining inventory, invoicing, customer debt tracking, team management, and reporting in a single app with a full Arabic interface. The multi-company architecture lets owners manage multiple stores or warehouses under one account, while the granular permission system ensures employees only access what they need. The app is free to download with a subscription model for premium features.
