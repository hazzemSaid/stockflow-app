## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our app:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase** | Primary backend — authentication, database, file storage, serverless functions | All account, company, product, customer, and invoice data |

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- **Data at rest:** All data is stored securely on Supabase servers with encryption at rest.
- **Data in transit:** All communications between the app and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS.
- **Authentication:** Passwords are hashed and managed securely by Supabase Auth.
- **Local storage:** Sensitive data on your device is stored using `flutter_secure_storage`, which uses the platform's native keystore (Keychain on iOS, EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android).
- **Image storage:** All uploaded images are stored in Supabase Storage with unique identifiers.
